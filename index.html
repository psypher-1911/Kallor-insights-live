Inference
What is happening

CTD is trading for the first time since August 2025.

Why

While it was suspended, CTD was thrown out of the ASX 200 and ASX 300. The funds that track those indexes were not allowed to sell then — so they are selling now, whatever the price. Add funds that have been stuck for a year and must return cash. The business itself made $19.4m statutory net profit in FY26 (Appendix 4E)*, but carries a £118m UK restatement.

So what

Forced sellers explain today's volume; they explain the level only if the price bounces once they are done — after roughly 2–4 normal trading days. If it doesn't, ~$3 is the market's clearing valuation: about 4× FY26 underlying EBITDA of $113.6m, which looks cheap until you put the debt, remediation cash and interest ahead of the equity. The metric that matters now is post-remediation, post-interest free cash flow. Meanwhile the $175m PEP Credit loan now equals a large share of the whole company's market value, its ≈$20m-a-year interest is more than FY26's entire $19.4m profit, and getting out of it costs 4% of the market cap at the time (≈$19m today, $80m+ if the shares recover first) — the incentive is to refinance while cheap.

What would change the view

If heavy selling continues after the index money should have cleared — or the price simply sits at $3 on falling volume — it's a real repricing, not forced flow. Institutional buying establishing a floor, FY27 cash-flow/remediation guidance, and any confirmed board reset are the next thesis-changers. Good signs: volume falling day by day, price climbing back above VWAP, strong closing auctions, and holder notices naming index funds.
