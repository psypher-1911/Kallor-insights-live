Inference
What is happening

CTD is trading for the first time since August 2025.

Why

While it was suspended, CTD was thrown out of the ASX 200 and ASX 300. The funds that track those indexes were not allowed to sell then — so they are selling now, whatever the price. Add funds that have been stuck for a year and must return cash. The business itself made $17.7m in FY26, but carries a £118m UK restatement.

So what

Today's price says more about who has to sell than what CTD is worth. Expect the real price to emerge after roughly 2–4 normal trading days.

What would change the view

If heavy selling continues after the index money should have cleared, it's a real repricing, not forced flow. Good signs: volume falling day by day, price climbing back above VWAP, strong closing auctions, and holder notices naming index funds.
